## 1. Benefits Eligibility

### 1.1 Eligibility Requirements
Benefits are available to full-time employees (40 hours/week) after a 30-day waiting period from their start date. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the waiting period completion.

**Example:** An employee starting on March 15 completes their waiting period on April 14. Coverage begins May 1.

Part-time employees working 30+ hours per week are eligible for medical, dental, and vision insurance only. They are not eligible for life insurance, disability, HSA employer contributions, or equity grants.

### 1.2 Dependent Coverage
Employees may enroll eligible dependents including:
- Legal spouse or registered domestic partner
- Biological, adopted, or step-children up to age 26
- Children up to age 26 placed for adoption (documentation required)

Domestic partner coverage requires completion of an Affidavit of Domestic Partnership, available from the People team.

### 1.3 Open Enrollment
Open enrollment occurs annually in November for coverage effective January 1. Outside of open enrollment, changes to benefits elections may only be made within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth/adoption, divorce, loss of other coverage, spouse's employment change).

## 2. Medical Insurance

### 2.1 Plan Options
NorthStar offers three medical plan options through Aetna:

**Plan A - PPO Premier**
- Monthly premium (employee only): $125
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $425
- Deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
- In-network coinsurance: 90/10
- Out-of-network coinsurance: 60/40
- Primary care visit copay: $20
- Specialist visit copay: $40
- Emergency room copay: $150 (waived if admitted)
- Prescription drugs: $10 generic / $35 preferred brand / $60 non-preferred brand

**Plan B - PPO Standard**
- Monthly premium (employee only): $75
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $275
- Deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- In-network coinsurance: 80/20
- Out-of-network coinsurance: 50/50
- Primary care visit copay: $30
- Specialist visit copay: $50
- Emergency room copay: $250 (waived if admitted)
- Prescription drugs: $15 generic / $45 preferred brand / $75 non-preferred brand

**Plan C - HDHP (High Deductible Health Plan)**
- Monthly premium (employee only): $35
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $150
- Deductible: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $6,500 individual / $13,000 family
- In-network coinsurance: 80/20 (after deductible)
- Out-of-network coinsurance: 50/50 (after deductible)
- No copays - all services subject to deductible
- Prescription drugs: Subject to deductible, then $10/$40/$70
- **Eligible for Health Savings Account (HSA) - see Section 5**

### 2.2 Telehealth
All medical plans include Teladoc telehealth services at no additional cost. Teladoc visits are $0 copay for Plan A and Plan B. For Plan C (HDHP), Teladoc visits are subject to the deductible until it is met, then $0 copay.

### 2.3 Mental Health Coverage
All plans include mental health coverage through Lyra Health:
- 12 free therapy sessions per year per covered individual (in-network Lyra providers only)
- After 12 sessions, standard in-network copays apply
- Psychiatric medication management visits have the same copay as specialist visits
- Crisis support available 24/7 through Lyra's crisis line

### 2.4 Fertility Benefits
Available to employees enrolled in Plan A or Plan B with at least 1 year of tenure:
- Up to $25,000 lifetime benefit for fertility treatments (IVF, IUI, egg freezing)
- Covers diagnostic testing, medication (up to 50% of medication costs), and procedures
- Requires pre-authorization from Aetna Fertility Services
- Not available under Plan C (HDHP)

## 3. Dental Insurance

NorthStar offers dental coverage through Delta Dental:

- Monthly premium (employee only): $25
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $75
- Preventive care (cleanings, X-rays): 100% covered, no deductible
- Basic procedures (fillings, extractions): 80% after $50 deductible
- Major procedures (crowns, bridges, root canals): 50% after $50 deductible
- Orthodontia (dependents under 19 only): 50% up to $2,000 lifetime maximum
- Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per covered individual

## 4. Vision Insurance

NorthStar offers vision coverage through VSP:

- Monthly premium (employee only): $10
- Monthly premium (employee + family): $30
- Eye exam: $10 copay, once per 12 months
- Frames: $150 allowance, once per 24 months
- Lenses: $25 copay, once per 12 months
- Contact lenses (in lieu of frames): $150 allowance, once per 12 months
- Laser eye surgery: 15% discount through VSP network providers

## 5. Health Savings Account (HSA)

### 5.1 Eligibility
Only employees enrolled in Plan C (HDHP) are eligible for an HSA. Employees enrolled in Plan A or Plan B, or those covered by a spouse's non-HDHP plan, are not eligible.

### 5.2 Contribution Limits (2025)
- Individual coverage: $4,300 maximum annual contribution
- Family coverage: $8,550 maximum annual contribution
- Catch-up contribution (age 55+): Additional $1,000

### 5.3 Employer Contribution
NorthStar contributes to eligible employees' HSAs:
- Individual coverage: $750/year ($62.50/month), deposited bi-weekly
- Family coverage: $1,500/year ($125/month), deposited bi-weekly
- Employer contributions are prorated for employees who enroll mid-year

### 5.4 HSA Details
- HSA funds roll over year to year with no expiration
- Funds can be invested once the balance exceeds $1,000
- HSA remains with the employee if they leave NorthStar
- Administered through HealthEquity

## 6. Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

### 6.1 Healthcare FSA
Available to employees enrolled in Plan A or Plan B only (not compatible with HSA/Plan C).
- Maximum annual contribution: $3,300
- $610 carryover permitted to next plan year
- Use-it-or-lose-it: Remaining balance above $610 is forfeited on March 31 of the following year

### 6.2 Dependent Care FSA
Available to all full-time employees regardless of medical plan:
- Maximum annual contribution: $5,000 (married filing jointly) or $2,500 (married filing separately)
- Eligible expenses: Daycare, preschool, before/after school care, summer day camp for dependents under 13
- Overnight camps are not eligible
- No carryover - strict use-it-or-lose-it by March 31 of the following year

## 7. Retirement Benefits

### 7.1 401(k) Plan
- Administered through Fidelity
- Eligible after 30 days of employment (same as benefits waiting period)
- Employee contribution: Up to IRS maximum ($23,500 for 2025; $31,000 for employees age 50+)
- **Employer match: 100% match on first 4% of salary, then 50% match on next 2% of salary (maximum employer contribution: 5% of salary)**
- Vesting schedule for employer match: 25% per year of service, fully vested after 4 years
- Immediate vesting for employees at Director level (Level 7) and above

Part-time employees are eligible for the 401(k) plan after 6 months of employment with the same matching formula.

### 7.2 Investment Options
The 401(k) plan offers target-date funds, index funds, bond funds, and a self-directed brokerage option. A Roth 401(k) option is also available with the same contribution limits and employer match.

## 8. Life and Disability Insurance

### 8.1 Basic Life Insurance
- Provided at no cost to full-time employees
- Coverage: 2x annual base salary (up to $500,000 maximum)
- Includes Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) coverage at the same amount

### 8.2 Supplemental Life Insurance
- Optional coverage up to 5x annual salary (up to $1,000,000)
- Employee-paid through payroll deduction
- Coverage above 3x salary requires medical underwriting (Evidence of Insurability)
- Spouse coverage available: Up to $100,000 (increments of $25,000)
- Child coverage available: $10,000 per child

### 8.3 Short-Term Disability
- Provided at no cost to full-time employees
- Waiting period: 7 calendar days
- Benefit: 60% of base salary, up to $2,500/week
- Maximum duration: 12 weeks
- Runs concurrently with FMLA leave if applicable (see Leave of Absence Policy HR-LOA-001)

### 8.4 Long-Term Disability
- Provided at no cost to full-time employees
- Waiting period: 90 calendar days (begins after short-term disability exhaustion)
- Benefit: 60% of base salary, up to $15,000/month
- Maximum duration: Until age 65 or Social Security Normal Retirement Age
- Pre-existing condition limitation: 12-month look-back, 12-month exclusion period

## 9. Additional Benefits

### 9.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees (including part-time and temporary) have access to the EAP through ComPsych:
- 6 free counseling sessions per issue per year
- Legal consultation (30-minute initial consult free)
- Financial planning consultation (free initial session)
- Work-life referrals (childcare, eldercare, moving assistance)
- Available 24/7 by phone and online

Note: EAP counseling sessions are separate from the Lyra Health therapy benefit under medical insurance. Employees can use both programs.

### 9.2 Commuter Benefits
Pre-tax commuter benefits through WageWorks:
- Transit: Up to $325/month pre-tax
- Parking: Up to $325/month pre-tax
- Not available to fully remote employees

### 9.3 Wellness Program
- Annual wellness reimbursement: $600/year for gym memberships, fitness classes, wellness apps, or sports league fees
- Must submit receipts through Concur within 60 days of purchase
- Wellness reimbursement is taxable income

### 9.4 Pet Insurance
Voluntary pet insurance through Nationwide at group rates. 100% employee-paid. Enrollment available during open enrollment or within 30 days of hire.

### 9.5 Student Loan Repayment Assistance
Available to full-time employees after 1 year of tenure:
- NorthStar contributes $100/month toward eligible federal or private student loans
- Maximum lifetime benefit: $10,000
- Employee must be actively making payments on the loan
- Benefit ceases upon termination of employment (no repayment required for amounts already received)

## 10. Benefits Upon Termination

### 10.1 COBRA
Upon termination, employees may continue medical, dental, and vision coverage for up to 18 months (36 months for dependents in certain circumstances) through COBRA. COBRA premiums are 102% of the full plan cost (employee + employer share).

### 10.2 Severance
Severance is not guaranteed and is provided at the company's discretion. When offered, the standard severance package is:
- 2 weeks of base salary per year of service (minimum 4 weeks, maximum 26 weeks)
- Continuation of medical benefits at employee premium rates during the severance period
- Outplacement services for 3 months
- Requires execution of a separation agreement including a general release of claims

### 10.3 Unused PTO
For employees with accrued PTO (below Senior level), unused PTO is paid out upon termination in states where required by law. For employees on unlimited PTO (Senior level and above), no PTO payout is provided upon termination.
