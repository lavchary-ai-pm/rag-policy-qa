# NorthStar Labs - Leave of Absence Policy

**Document ID:** HR-LOA-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** January 15, 2025
**Approved By:** Sarah Chen, Chief People Officer

---

## 1. Overview

This policy covers extended leaves of absence beyond standard PTO and sick leave described in the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001). All leave requests should be submitted through Workday and coordinated with the People team.

---

## 2. Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

### 2.1 Eligibility
Employees are eligible for FMLA leave if they have:
- Worked at NorthStar for at least 12 months (does not need to be consecutive)
- Worked at least 1,250 hours during the 12 months preceding the leave
- Work at a location where NorthStar employs 50 or more employees within 75 miles

### 2.2 Covered Reasons
- Birth and care of a newborn child (within 12 months of birth)
- Placement of a child for adoption or foster care (within 12 months of placement)
- Care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition
- The employee's own serious health condition that prevents them from performing their job
- Qualifying exigency arising from a family member's military service
- Care for a covered service member with a serious injury or illness (up to 26 weeks)

### 2.3 Duration and Pay
- FMLA provides up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave per 12-month period (rolling backward)
- FMLA leave is unpaid by default, BUT NorthStar requires employees to use accrued PTO and sick leave concurrently with FMLA leave until those balances are exhausted
- Employees on unlimited PTO (Senior level and above) will receive base pay for the first 4 weeks of FMLA leave, after which the leave becomes unpaid unless supplemented by short-term disability
- Short-term disability runs concurrently with FMLA when applicable (see Benefits Guide HR-BEN-001 Section 8.3)

### 2.4 Benefits During FMLA
- Health insurance coverage continues during FMLA leave at the same employee premium rate
- If the employee fails to return from FMLA leave (except due to continuation of a serious health condition), NorthStar may recover the employer-paid portion of health insurance premiums during the leave period

## 3. Parental Leave

### 3.1 Birth Parent Leave
NorthStar provides paid parental leave beyond what FMLA requires:
- **16 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base salary** for the birth parent
- This includes any FMLA entitlement and short-term disability benefits (NorthStar "tops up" the difference so total pay equals 100% of base salary)
- Additional unpaid leave up to 8 weeks may be requested on a case-by-case basis
- Must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth
- May be taken continuously or in two separate blocks (minimum 2 weeks per block)

### 3.2 Non-Birth Parent Leave
- **8 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base salary** for the non-birth parent (including adoptive and foster parents)
- Must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth, adoption, or foster placement
- May be taken continuously or in two separate blocks (minimum 2 weeks per block)

### 3.3 Adoption and Foster Care Leave
- Adoptive parents receive the same leave as non-birth parents: 8 weeks paid at 100% of base salary
- If only one parent works at NorthStar, the primary caregiver may request birth parent leave duration (16 weeks) with VP and People team approval
- Pre-adoption leave: Up to 5 days of paid leave for adoption-related appointments, travel, and court proceedings (does not count against the 8-week parental leave)

### 3.4 Eligibility
- Parental leave benefits are available to full-time employees who have been employed for at least 6 months at the time of the birth, adoption, or placement
- Employees with less than 6 months of tenure receive FMLA-only benefits (if FMLA-eligible) or up to 4 weeks of unpaid leave

### 3.5 Returning from Parental Leave
- Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed the same or an equivalent position
- Employees who decide not to return must notify the People team at least 2 weeks before their scheduled return date and must repay any parental leave pay received beyond what FMLA would have provided
- A phased return (reduced hours for up to 4 weeks) may be arranged with manager approval

## 4. Medical Leave (Non-FMLA)

### 4.1 Extended Medical Leave
Employees who have exhausted FMLA leave or are not FMLA-eligible may request extended medical leave for up to an additional 12 weeks. This leave is:
- Unpaid (unless the employee has accrued PTO or sick leave remaining)
- Subject to approval by the People team and the employee's VP
- Requires medical certification from the employee's healthcare provider
- Job protection is not guaranteed but will be provided when feasible

### 4.2 Intermittent Medical Leave
For chronic conditions requiring periodic time off, employees may request intermittent FMLA leave. This allows the employee to take leave in separate blocks or reduce their work schedule. Intermittent leave:
- Requires medical certification specifying the expected frequency and duration of absences
- Must be scheduled to minimize disruption when foreseeable
- May result in temporary transfer to an equivalent position that better accommodates intermittent absences

## 5. Bereavement Leave

### 5.1 Immediate Family
Employees receive up to 5 consecutive paid business days for the death of an immediate family member:
- Spouse or domestic partner
- Child (biological, adopted, step, foster)
- Parent (biological, adoptive, step, foster, in-law)
- Sibling (biological, adopted, step)
- Grandparent or grandchild

### 5.2 Extended Family and Close Friends
Employees receive up to 3 consecutive paid business days for the death of:
- Aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin
- Close friend (at manager's discretion)

### 5.3 Additional Time
If additional time is needed beyond the bereavement leave allowance, employees may use PTO, sick leave, or request unpaid time off. In cases of unusual circumstances (e.g., international travel required, estate management responsibilities), employees may request up to 5 additional paid bereavement days through the People team.

### 5.4 Pregnancy or Infant Loss
In the event of a miscarriage, stillbirth, or infant loss, the birth parent is eligible for up to 4 weeks of paid leave. The non-birth parent is eligible for up to 2 weeks of paid leave. This leave is separate from bereavement leave and may be followed by additional medical leave if needed.

## 6. Jury Duty and Legal Leave

### 6.1 Jury Duty
Employees summoned for jury duty will receive full pay for up to 10 business days per year. If jury service extends beyond 10 days, the employee will receive the difference between jury pay and their regular base salary for up to an additional 20 business days. Employees must provide a copy of the jury summons and notify their manager as soon as possible.

### 6.2 Witness Duty
Employees subpoenaed as witnesses (not as a party to the case) receive up to 3 paid days off per occurrence.

### 6.3 Voting
Employees are provided up to 2 hours of paid time off to vote in federal, state, or local elections if their work schedule does not allow sufficient time before or after work.

---

## 7. Military Leave

### 7.1 Active Duty
Employees called to active military duty receive the difference between their military pay and NorthStar base salary for up to 12 months. Benefits continue during military leave for up to 12 months. Upon return, employees are entitled to reemployment in accordance with USERRA.

### 7.2 Reserve/Guard Training
Employees who are members of military reserves or the National Guard receive up to 15 paid days per year for required training. Additional training days are unpaid but job-protected.

---

## 8. Sabbatical Leave

### 8.1 Eligibility
Full-time employees with 7 or more continuous years of service at NorthStar are eligible for a sabbatical leave.

### 8.2 Duration and Pay
- 4 weeks of paid sabbatical at 100% of base salary
- An additional 2-4 weeks of unpaid sabbatical may be approved
- Sabbatical must be taken in a single continuous block
- Employees may not combine sabbatical with PTO to extend the paid period beyond 4 weeks

### 8.3 Approval and Planning
- Sabbatical requests must be submitted at least 6 months in advance
- Approval is based on business needs and team coverage
- A second sabbatical may be taken after an additional 7 years of continuous service

---

## 9. Personal Leave of Absence

### 9.1 Availability
Employees may request an unpaid personal leave of absence for reasons not covered by other leave categories (extended travel, caregiving for non-immediate family, personal projects, education).

### 9.2 Terms
- Maximum duration: 30 calendar days per year
- Requires manager and VP approval
- Benefits continue if the employee pays the full premium (employee + employer share)
- Job protection is not guaranteed but will be provided when feasible
- Not available during the employee's first year of employment

---

## 10. Return from Leave

### 10.1 Notification
Employees must confirm their return date with the People team at least 5 business days before returning from any leave exceeding 2 weeks.

### 10.2 Fitness for Duty
For medical leaves, a fitness-for-duty certification from the employee's healthcare provider may be required before returning to work.

### 10.3 Accommodations
Employees returning from leave who need workplace accommodations should contact the People team to begin the interactive accommodation process. NorthStar provides reasonable accommodations in accordance with the ADA and applicable state laws.
