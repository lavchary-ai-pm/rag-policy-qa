## 1. Welcome to NorthStar Labs

NorthStar Labs is a B2B SaaS company providing enterprise data integration solutions. This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees across our offices in Austin (HQ), Denver, and New York, as well as fully remote employees in approved US states.

This handbook should be read in conjunction with the Benefits Guide (HR-BEN-001), Leave of Absence Policy (HR-LOA-001), Information Security Policy (IT-SEC-001), Code of Conduct (HR-COC-001), and Performance & Compensation Policy (HR-PER-001).

---

## 2. Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
Employees regularly scheduled to work 40 hours per week. Full-time employees are eligible for all benefits described in the Benefits Guide (HR-BEN-001) after completing the 30-day waiting period.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees
Employees regularly scheduled to work between 20 and 39 hours per week. Part-time employees working 30+ hours per week are eligible for health insurance benefits only. Part-time employees working fewer than 30 hours are not eligible for company-sponsored benefits but may participate in the 401(k) plan after 6 months of employment.

### 2.3 Temporary/Contract Employees
Employees hired for a specific project or time period not exceeding 12 months. Temporary employees are not eligible for benefits, PTO accrual, or equity grants. Temporary employees who convert to full-time status begin their benefits waiting period on the conversion date.

### 2.4 Interns
Paid internship positions lasting 10-12 weeks. Interns receive a housing stipend of $2,000/month for non-local interns. Interns are not eligible for benefits or PTO but receive 3 personal days during their internship.

## 3. Work Arrangements

### 3.1 Hybrid Work Policy
NorthStar operates on a hybrid model. Employees within 50 miles of an office are expected to work in-office a minimum of 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are designated anchor days). Managers may require additional in-office days based on team needs with 2 weeks advance notice.

Exceptions: Engineering teams on the Platform Infrastructure group follow a 3-day in-office schedule (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) due to hardware lab access requirements.

### 3.2 Fully Remote Work
Employees may be approved for fully remote work if they:
- Live more than 50 miles from the nearest NorthStar office, OR
- Have received written approval from their VP and the People team
- Reside in a state where NorthStar has employment registration (see Appendix A for approved states list)

Fully remote employees must maintain a dedicated home office that meets ergonomic standards. NorthStar provides a one-time $1,500 home office setup stipend for newly approved remote workers. This stipend is not available to employees who were hired as remote from their start date - they receive the stipend as part of their onboarding package instead.

### 3.3 Temporary Remote Work
Employees may request up to 4 weeks of temporary remote work per calendar year for personal reasons (e.g., caring for a family member, home renovation). Requests must be approved by the direct manager and do not require VP approval. Temporary remote work from outside the US is not permitted due to tax and compliance requirements.

### 3.4 Work Hours and Flexibility
Core business hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. Employees are expected to be available during core hours for meetings and collaboration. Start and end times outside core hours are flexible.

Employees in customer-facing roles (Sales, Customer Success, Support) may have modified core hours based on their assigned territory. See your manager for territory-specific schedules.

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 4.1 PTO Accrual
NorthStar offers an unlimited PTO policy for all full-time employees at the Senior level (Level 5) and above. Employees below Senior level accrue PTO as follows:

| Tenure | Annual PTO Days | Max Carryover |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| 0-2 years | 15 days | 5 days |
| 2-5 years | 20 days | 8 days |
| 5+ years | 25 days | 10 days |

PTO accrues on a per-pay-period basis (bi-weekly). New employees begin accruing PTO on their first day but may not use PTO during their first 90 days except in emergencies approved by their manager.

### 4.2 PTO Request Process
- Requests of 1-3 days: Notify your manager at least 1 week in advance
- Requests of 4-10 days: Submit through Workday at least 3 weeks in advance
- Requests exceeding 10 consecutive days: Require manager and VP approval at least 6 weeks in advance. Requests of this length may not be taken during Q4 (October-December) for revenue-generating roles.

### 4.3 Company Holidays
NorthStar observes 10 paid holidays per year:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

Additionally, employees receive 2 floating holidays per year to use for personal, cultural, or religious observances. Floating holidays do not carry over and must be used within the calendar year.

### 4.4 Sick Leave
Full-time employees receive 10 sick days per year, separate from PTO. Unused sick days do not carry over. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent). Absences exceeding 3 consecutive sick days require a doctor's note.

For extended medical leave, refer to the Leave of Absence Policy (HR-LOA-001).

## 5. Expense Policy

### 5.1 Business Travel
All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager. Travel exceeding $3,000 requires VP approval. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip through Concur.

**Per diem rates:**
- Domestic travel: $75/day for meals and incidentals
- International travel: Rates follow the US State Department per diem schedule for the destination city

**Hotel policy:** Employees should book hotels at or below the GSA rate for the destination city. Exceptions for high-cost cities (San Francisco, New York, Seattle, Boston) allow up to 150% of the GSA rate.

**Air travel:** Economy class is standard for all domestic flights. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 6 hours. First class travel is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

### 5.2 Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable up to $150 per person per event. Events exceeding $500 total require pre-approval from a Director or above. All client entertainment expenses must include the names of attendees and the business purpose.

### 5.3 Professional Development
Each employee receives an annual professional development budget of $2,500. This covers conferences, courses, books, and certifications. Unused professional development funds do not carry over. Conference attendance requires manager approval and should not conflict with critical project milestones.

Employees at the Director level and above receive an enhanced professional development budget of $5,000 per year. See the Performance & Compensation Policy (HR-PER-001) for details on tuition reimbursement for degree programs.

### 5.4 Home Office Equipment (Remote Workers)
In addition to the one-time setup stipend (Section 3.2), remote workers may expense up to $500/year for home office equipment replacement (monitors, keyboards, chairs, etc.). This is separate from the IT equipment provided by the company (laptop, docking station).

## 6. Communication and Tools

### 6.1 Approved Communication Tools
- **Slack:** Primary internal communication. Channels must follow the naming convention defined by IT.
- **Zoom:** Video conferencing for internal and external meetings.
- **Email (Google Workspace):** Official business communication and external correspondence.
- **Confluence:** Internal documentation and knowledge base.
- **Jira:** Engineering project tracking.

Use of unapproved messaging tools (WhatsApp, iMessage, personal email) for business communication is prohibited per the Information Security Policy (IT-SEC-001).

### 6.2 Meeting-Free Zones
Wednesdays are designated as "Focus Days" with no recurring meetings permitted. One-off meetings on Wednesdays are discouraged but allowed for time-sensitive matters. All-hands meetings and company-wide events are exempt from this policy.

## Appendix A: Approved Remote Work States

NorthStar Labs has employment registration in the following states: Arizona, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Massachusetts, Minnesota, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Texas, Utah, Virginia, Washington.

Employees requesting remote work from states not on this list must consult with the People team at least 60 days before the intended move. Registration in new states requires legal and tax review and is not guaranteed.
