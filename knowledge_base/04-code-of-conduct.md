# NorthStar Labs - Code of Conduct and Ethics

**Document ID:** HR-COC-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Approved By:** David Park, General Counsel & Sarah Chen, Chief People Officer

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes ethical standards and behavioral expectations for all NorthStar Labs employees, officers, directors, contractors, and temporary workers. Every employee must acknowledge and agree to this Code upon hire and annually during the compliance certification process.

---

## 2. Core Values and Expected Behavior

### 2.1 Respect and Inclusion
NorthStar is committed to maintaining a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. We do not tolerate discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, or any other characteristic protected by law.

### 2.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
Harassment includes any unwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment. This includes but is not limited to:
- Verbal harassment: slurs, jokes, derogatory comments, threats
- Physical harassment: unwanted touching, blocking movement, physical intimidation
- Visual harassment: offensive images, cartoons, or gestures
- Sexual harassment: unwanted sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexual comments
- Cyber harassment: harassing behavior through Slack, email, social media, or other digital channels

Harassment by or against anyone in the workplace is prohibited, including behavior directed at employees, contractors, vendors, customers, and candidates.

### 2.3 Reporting Harassment or Discrimination
Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it through any of the following channels:
- Direct manager (unless the manager is involved in the conduct)
- Any member of the People team
- The Ethics Hotline: 1-888-555-ETHC (anonymous reporting available)
- Email: ethics@northstarlabs.com
- Online: ethics.northstarlabs.com (anonymous web form available)

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly, typically within 10 business days. Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is strictly prohibited and is grounds for immediate termination.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

### 3.1 Definition
A conflict of interest exists when an employee's personal interests, relationships, or activities could interfere with their duty to act in NorthStar's best interest.

### 3.2 Disclosure Requirements
Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest to their manager and the Legal team, including:
- Financial interests (ownership, investment, or financial stake) in any NorthStar competitor, customer, or vendor
- Employment or consulting arrangements with any other company (see Section 3.3)
- Romantic or familial relationships with NorthStar employees, contractors, customers, or vendors where one party has influence over the other's employment, compensation, or business dealings
- Board positions or advisory roles with external organizations

Disclosures must be submitted through the Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form (available on the People team's Confluence page) within 30 days of the conflict arising or the employee becoming aware of it.

### 3.3 Outside Employment and Activities
Employees may hold outside employment or engage in freelance work provided that:
- It does not interfere with their NorthStar responsibilities or work hours
- It does not compete with NorthStar's business
- It does not use NorthStar's confidential information, resources, or relationships
- It is disclosed to and approved by their manager and the Legal team

Employees at the Director level (Level 7) and above must obtain written approval from the CEO for any outside board positions, advisory roles, or compensated speaking engagements.

### 3.4 Investment Restrictions
Employees with access to material non-public information about NorthStar's financial performance, M&A activity, or significant business developments are subject to the Insider Trading Policy and must obtain pre-clearance from the General Counsel before trading NorthStar securities. This applies to all employees at the VP level and above, all members of the Finance team, and anyone designated as an "insider" by the General Counsel.

## 4. Gifts and Entertainment

### 4.1 Receiving Gifts
Employees may accept gifts from vendors, customers, or business partners only if:
- The gift is valued at $100 or less per occasion
- The gift is not cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, cryptocurrency)
- The gift does not create an expectation of reciprocity
- The gift is disclosed to your manager

Gifts valued between $100 and $250 require written approval from a VP. Gifts valued above $250 must be declined or turned over to the People team for donation to the company's charitable giving program.

During vendor selection processes (RFPs, contract renewals), no gifts of any value may be accepted from participating vendors.

### 4.2 Giving Gifts
Gifts to government officials or employees of public entities are prohibited under all circumstances due to anti-bribery laws. Gifts to customers or partners must comply with their organization's gift policies when known and must not exceed $200 per recipient per year. All gifts given must be reported through Concur expense reporting.

### 4.3 Entertainment
Business meals and entertainment with clients, vendors, or partners should be reasonable and proportionate to the business relationship. See the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001) Section 5.2 for expense limits. Entertainment that could be perceived as excessive or inappropriate (nightclubs, gambling, adult entertainment) is never permissible as a business expense.

## 5. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

### 5.1 Confidential Information
Employees must protect NorthStar's confidential information during and after employment. Confidential information includes all non-public information about NorthStar's business, technology, customers, finances, and strategies. See the Information Security Policy (IT-SEC-001) Section 2 for data classification details.

### 5.2 Intellectual Property
All work product created by employees within the scope of their employment is the property of NorthStar Labs, as outlined in the Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (PIIA) signed at hire.

Employees who wish to contribute to open-source projects must obtain approval from Engineering leadership and Legal. Contributions must not include NorthStar proprietary code or reveal NorthStar's technology strategy.

### 5.3 Post-Employment Obligations
Upon leaving NorthStar, employees must:
- Return all company property, documents, and data
- Continue to protect confidential information indefinitely
- Comply with any non-compete, non-solicitation, or non-disclosure agreements
- Not solicit NorthStar employees or customers for 12 months (for employees at Senior level and above, as specified in their employment agreement)

## 6. Anti-Corruption and Bribery

### 6.1 Policy
NorthStar prohibits bribery, kickbacks, and corrupt payments in all forms. This policy applies globally, regardless of local customs or practices. Violations may result in termination and referral to law enforcement.

### 6.2 Facilitation Payments
Facilitation payments (small payments to expedite routine government actions) are prohibited, even in countries where they may be customary or legal.

### 6.3 Third-Party Due Diligence
Employees who engage third-party agents, consultants, or distributors in markets with heightened corruption risk must complete the Third-Party Due Diligence process through the Legal team before entering into any agreement.

## 7. Workplace Safety

### 7.1 Drug-Free Workplace
NorthStar maintains a drug-free workplace. The use, possession, distribution, or being under the influence of illegal drugs on company premises or during work hours is prohibited. Prescription medications that may impair work performance should be discussed with your manager (disclosure of the specific medication is not required, only potential impact on duties).

Alcohol is permitted at company-sponsored events in moderation. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and must not operate vehicles or company equipment while impaired.

### 7.2 Weapons
Weapons of any kind (firearms, knives with blades over 3 inches, explosives, or other dangerous items) are prohibited on NorthStar premises, including parking areas, regardless of any permit to carry.

### 7.3 Workplace Violence
Threats of violence, intimidation, or actual violence are prohibited and will result in immediate termination and referral to law enforcement. Employees who feel unsafe should contact their manager, the People team, or local law enforcement immediately.

## 8. Social Media and Public Statements

### 8.1 Personal Social Media
Employees may use personal social media but must:
- Not disclose confidential information about NorthStar's business, products, or customers
- Not speak on behalf of NorthStar unless authorized by the Communications team
- Include a disclaimer that views are their own when discussing industry topics related to NorthStar's business
- Not disparage NorthStar, its employees, customers, or competitors

### 8.2 Company Social Media
Only employees authorized by the Marketing team may post on NorthStar's official social media accounts. All official social media content must be reviewed by Marketing before posting.

### 8.3 Media and Analyst Inquiries
All media inquiries must be directed to the Communications team (press@northstarlabs.com). Employees may not speak to media about NorthStar without prior approval from Communications. Industry analyst interactions are coordinated by the Product Marketing team.

## 9. Reporting Violations and Whistleblower Protection

### 9.1 Obligation to Report
All employees have an obligation to report known or suspected violations of this Code, applicable laws, or company policies. Reports can be made through:
- Direct manager
- People team
- Legal team
- Ethics Hotline: 1-888-555-ETHC (anonymous)
- Email: ethics@northstarlabs.com
- Web: ethics.northstarlabs.com (anonymous)

### 9.2 Investigation Process
All reports are investigated by the appropriate team (People, Legal, or IT Security depending on the nature of the report). Investigations are conducted confidentially to the extent possible. The reporting employee will be notified of the outcome to the extent permitted by privacy requirements.

### 9.3 Whistleblower Protection
NorthStar will not retaliate against any employee who reports a violation in good faith, cooperates with an investigation, or refuses to participate in activity they reasonably believe to be illegal or unethical. This protection extends to employees who report to external authorities (SEC, DOJ, etc.).

### 9.4 Consequences of Violations
Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action including:
- Verbal or written warning
- Mandatory additional training
- Suspension without pay
- Demotion or reassignment
- Termination of employment
- Referral to law enforcement (for criminal violations)

The severity of consequences will be proportional to the nature and severity of the violation, the employee's intent, prior violations, and any mitigating circumstances.

## 10. Annual Certification

All employees must complete the annual Code of Conduct certification by December 31 of each year. This includes:
- Reviewing the current Code of Conduct
- Completing the online compliance training (approximately 45 minutes)
- Certifying that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with the Code
- Disclosing any potential conflicts of interest

Non-completion of annual certification by the deadline will result in restricted access to certain systems until completed and will be noted in the employee's performance review.
